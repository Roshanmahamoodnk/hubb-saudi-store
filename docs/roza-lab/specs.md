## HVAC and extract

- Keep existing split AC on the corridor return (P01/P02/P09). Do not block with the TV wall.
- Keep Crompton wall fans in the sunroom as air movement only — **not** grease extract.
- **New:** dedicated extract for the sample roaster / cooktop. Duct to exterior; **make-up air** so the hood does not steal corridor AC.
- Do not vent cooking into the sunroom or CEO office.
- If the building forbids a new flue, specify a recirculating hood with charcoal **only** as a temporary measure and note the limitation.

- Wet / dry split: no roast cooling on Sangeetha’s paperwork desk.

- Hand-wash at the existing sink before it is used as a food sink, **or** add a separate hand-wash if the authority requires it.
- Sample jars: closed lids; no open green-bean bags on the CEO desk after Phase 2.
