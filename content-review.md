## FAQ — usable answers

**What is HUBB? / ما هو حُبّ؟**  
HUBB is flavoured in-shell sunflower seeds, available in four flavours and several pack formats.  
حُبّ بذور دوّار الشمس المنكّهة بقشرها، بأربع نكهات وعدّة أحجام للعبوات.

**Do I eat the shell? / هل آكل القشرة؟**  
No. Crack the husk, separate it from the kernel and eat only the kernel. Collect the discarded husks separately from your food.  
لا. اكسر القشرة وافصلها عن اللبّ، وكُل اللبّ فقط. اجمع القشور بعيدًا عن الطعام.

**What is inside the Journey Cup? / ماذا تحتوي عبوة الطلعة؟**  
Five sealed 30 g sachets of one flavour, plus a separate paper shell bag. Total net seed contents: 150 g.  
خمسة أكياس مغلقة، وزن كل منها ٣٠ غ، من نكهة واحدة، مع كيس ورقي منفصل للقشور. الوزن الصافي الإجمالي: ١٥٠ غ.

**What is inside the Family Mix? / ماذا تحتوي تشكيلة اللمّة؟**  
Twenty sealed 30 g sachets: five Classic Sea Salt, five Garlic Salt, five Pepper Lime and five Fire Salt. Total net seed contents: 600 g.  
عشرون كيسًا مغلقًا، وزن كل منها ٣٠ غ: خمسة ملح بحري، وخمسة ملح بالثوم، وخمسة فلفل ولايم، وخمسة ملح حار. الوزن الصافي الإجمالي: ٦٠٠ غ.

**Are the seeds loose inside the cup? / هل البذور سائبة داخل العبوة؟**  
No. The cup holds individually sealed sachets. Open one and keep the remaining sachets sealed.  
لا. تحتوي العبوة على أكياس مغلقة منفصلة. افتح كيسًا واحدًا واترك البقية مغلقة.

**Can I choose the flavours in the Family Mix? / هل أستطيع اختيار نكهات تشكيلة اللمّة؟**  
The current Family Mix contains all four flavours, with five sachets of each.  
تحتوي تشكيلة اللمّة الحالية على النكهات الأربع، بخمسة أكياس من كل نكهة.

**Where can I find ingredients and allergens? / أين أجد المكوّنات ومسبّبات الحساسية؟**  
The approved ingredient, allergen and nutrition information will appear on each product page and final pack before ordering becomes available.  
ستظهر معلومات المكوّنات ومسبّبات الحساسية والقيم الغذائية المعتمدة على صفحة كل منتج وعبوته النهائية قبل إتاحة الطلب.

**Can I place a real order in this preview? / هل يمكنني إجراء طلب فعلي في هذه المعاينة؟**  
This is a store preview. You can explore products and build a sample bag; payment and order fulfilment are not active.

## UGC and review integrity

- Use original generated lifestyle scenes as editorial brand concepts and label their collection “Concept scenes” or “مشاهد تصوّرية”. Do not use “Verified customer”, invented handles, customer quotes, fabricated engagement counts or star aggregates with generated people.
- Let submitted customer reviews start from an empty state. A demonstration form must say when the data is stored only on this device or is not sent. Do not make a successful-submission message claim the brand received content unless a real service accepted it.
- A future real review needs provenance, permission, moderation and an actual connection between the reviewer and their experience; a verified-purchase badge requires an order match.
- Concepts can show adult hands passing sachets at a contemporary Saudi home, at a parked roadside stop, or between rounds with controllers resting. Keep skin, sleeves, objects and seed scale physically plausible. No faces are needed for the established direction.
- The Howcast tutorial and extracted GIFs are reference/analysis material. No commercial reuse licence is established; do not embed them as HUBB product advertising, UGC or branded tutorial footage.

## Creative critique and implementation priorities

**Overall impression:** Ivory, sand and cocoa fit the current pack system and give the product room to feel tactile. The strongest cultural connection is the everyday act of bringing, opening and passing a sachet; it should shape the photography and copy more than decorative motifs.

| Finding | Priority | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| Ivory editorial layouts can read like fragrance or skincare. | High | Put the in-shell seed category and a legible pack above the fold. Use a believable cracked shell and kernel as a small food cue. |
| A full-screen decorative hero can hide shopping. | High | Keep a clear “Find your flavour” CTA and a nearby exact format/count descriptor. Let mobile users reach the first product in one short scroll. |
| Generated Arabic on each SKU can vary or distort. | High | Establish one original master mark and use live typeset labels wherever possible. Do not create a new wordmark independently for every image. |
| A cream pack on a cream page may lose its silhouette. | Medium | Use controlled warm shadows, slight background tone separation and sufficient object scale; avoid gray cutout rectangles. |
| Four broad brush colours on the family bucket undermine V5 restraint. | High | Keep the family outer ivory and brown; show four small sachet pictures. Confine the colour differences to the contents. |
| Decorative heritage motifs can imply unsupported authenticity. | Medium | Use an original restrained woven rhythm. Avoid “authentic Al Sadu”, an artist credit, heritage certification, Saudi crop origin or artisan-made claims unless substantiated. |
| Beige-on-beige text is difficult to read. | High | Use cocoa for primary text, preserve contrast for small labels, and use colour plus words for flavour choices. Check rendered contrast rather than assuming the palette passes. |
| Motion may obscure product selection. | Medium | Use restrained pack rotation, brush reveals and section transitions. Respect reduced-motion preference and avoid scroll capture or forced loading animations. |
| Arabic may become a decorative second language. | High | Make the Arabic switch a complete RTL experience: navigation, product details, cart, controls and errors. Keep Latin product names and numbers readable using bidirectional isolation. |
| A fake social-proof section undermines credibility. | High | Build a polished empty review state and use editorial moment cards until authentic submissions exist. |

Priority order: coherent master packaging → clear product/category recognition → fast bilingual shopping → honest original moment scenes → restrained motion polish. “Award-calibre” is the ambition; no result, nomination or affiliation is represented as achieved.
