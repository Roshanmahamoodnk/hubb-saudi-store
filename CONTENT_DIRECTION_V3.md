# HUBB V3 — A little HUBB. Happiness to share.

6 September 2026. Current owner direction supersedes the R&D-led V2 homepage opening. Consumer Arabic remains draft pending Saudi copywriter validation. This document records the implementation intent, not a claim of launch validation.

The customer should meet a familiar human moment before meeting the manufacturing story. The first screen now connects flavour inside the seed with a small pleasure shared: a pack opened, a favourite chosen, a conversation that continues. “About HUBB” carries the two-year effort later in the journey, after the products and imagined occasions have made its relevance clear.

## Page sequence and the job of each section

1. **The invitation:** “A little HUBB. Happiness to share.” / “حبّة حُبّ. ولمّة تحلى.” The category and Saudi manufacture are clear immediately. Shop the flavours or discover a sharing moment.
2. **Choose your flavour and actual format:** four flavours shown as four cups or four cartons, according to the selected format. Both format and quantity stay consistent in product detail, cart and order summary. There are eight flavour/format combinations, plus the existing Family Mix; no invented fifth flavour.
3. **Make room for everyone:** Family Mix provides twenty sealed sachets, five of each flavour. Its emotional role is letting different people find their own favourite at the same gathering.
4. **Different days, familiar small happiness:** an illustrated creative campaign moves from football half-time to a work break, an intergenerational table and a parked road stop. The desktop photo changes with the chapter in view. Mobile shows each scene next to its own words.
5. **The ritual:** crack the shell, separate and eat only the kernel, collect husks separately. A silent scroll animation opens the seed, reveals the kernel and drops empty husks into a separate paper bag. No synthetic sound or click-to-advance control remains. Static instructions work without motion.
6. **About HUBB:** made in Saudi Arabia, two years of care, attention to roast/flavour/kernel/crack. Technical boiling history no longer leads the consumer story. The two-year account is supplied by the owner; no patent, certification, market leadership or quantified superiority is asserted.
7. **Let the customer speak:** ask which flavour they chose, whom they shared with, and how the crack and kernel felt. Product-tasting feedback stays distinct from concept feedback. WhatsApp is visitor-controlled; there are no invented ratings, testimonials or real customer posts.
8. **Build it together:** retailer, distributor and collaboration routes, then the exact supplied factory/warehouse locations and public contact.

## Motion intent

Photographs change while the visitor naturally scrolls the four moment chapters. Supporting motion consists of gentle entrances, pack hover movement, a reading-progress line and restrained image drift. Scroll speed is unchanged. No autoplayed audio, random testimonials, fake live activity, tracking-based personalisation or artificial wait is added. Visitors can jump directly to their moment using visible links.

Reduced-motion mode removes the sticky composition, parallax and animated transitions and shows a static two-column (one-column mobile) set of scenes. No information depends on motion.

Commercial policy is unchanged: no individual sachets online; proposed SAR39 goods minimum, delivery15 below75, free from75. Contact and checkout remain request/preview flows, not confirmed paid orders.
