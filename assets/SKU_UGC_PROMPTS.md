# HUBB SKU UGC posts — generation log

Created 2026-09-06. Original generated editorial concept imagery, using only the owner's HUBB pack references. Fictional adult models; these are not customer submissions, testimonials, endorsements or evidence of actual events. No social content was sent or published. No invented handles, likes, ratings or verified-purchase claims accompany the pictures on the website.

All outputs: 1024 × 1536 PNG masters and WebP derivatives at quality 88. WebP conversion only; no scene retouching. These are 2:3 portrait masters, usable with considered 3:4 crops.

Built-in image generation, one asset at a time, with explicit reference paths. Family Mix was generated twice after the first return included a third-party tissue brand; the second image is the published file. Generated small print can differ from catalogue facts; typeset page copy remains the source for counts and weights.
